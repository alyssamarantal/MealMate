Our Mission

MealMate was created to help people living in boarding houses, dorms, and homes find easy and budget-friendly recipes. We share simple cooking techniques and encourage thoughtful meal planning so delicious food can fit different budgets.

“Good food doesn't have to be expensive.
It just has to be delicious.”
