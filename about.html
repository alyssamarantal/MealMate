Our Mission

MealMate is a budget-friendly recipe website created to help people discover simple, affordable, and delicious meals they can prepare every day. We understand that cooking can sometimes be challenging, especially when you have a limited budget, a busy schedule, or only a few ingredients available in your kitchen.

Whether you are a student living in a dormitory, a boarder staying in a boarding house, a busy worker, or someone who simply wants to save money on food, MealMate is here to make everyday cooking easier and more accessible.

Our website features easy-to-follow recipes made with affordable and commonly available ingredients, such as eggs, noodles, canned goods, vegetables, and other everyday Filipino food staples. Each recipe includes ingredient lists, step-by-step cooking instructions, and estimated meal costs to help users plan their meals according to their budget.
